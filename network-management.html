Network Management

Network Management

We offer a wide range of network management services to help businesses keep their infrastructure secure, efficient, and reliable.

01
Network Design & Consulting
  • Designing scalable and resilient network architecture layouts (LAN, WAN, SD-WAN) tailored to a business's specific operational needs.
  • Assessing bandwidth capacity and planning for future growth to prevent system bottlenecks as the company scales.
  • Recommending and selecting the right networking hardware, including routers, switches, and wireless access points, based on budget and performance requirements.
02
Implementation & Deployment
  • Installing and connecting physical network infrastructure, which involves structured cabling and server racking.
  • Configuring routing protocols, VLANs, and subnets to securely segregate traffic and optimize data flow.
  • Deploying fast and secure wireless networks (Wi-Fi), utilizing heat mapping to determine the strategic placement of access points for maximum coverage.
03
Monitoring & Optimization
  • Providing 24/7 proactive monitoring using automated tools to track uptime, latency, and packet loss in real-time.
  • Managing traffic flow through Quality of Service (QoS) rules to prioritize bandwidth for critical business applications like voice and video communication.
  • Automatically identifying and isolating faults and failures before they cascade into widespread network downtime.
04
Network Security Implementation
  • Configuring and continuously managing firewalls to block unauthorized external access and filter dangerous traffic.
  • Deploying Intrusion Detection and Prevention Systems (IDPS) to monitor internal network activity for malware or unauthorized behavior.
  • Setting up secure, encrypted Virtual Private Networks (VPNs) to allow remote employees to safely access corporate resources from anywhere.
  • Enforcing strict Network Access Control (NAC) policies to ensure only verified and compliant devices can connect to the local network.
05
Maintenance & Routine Support
  • Executing routine patch management to keep the firmware and software of all network devices secure against newly discovered vulnerabilities.
  • Providing responsive troubleshooting and helpdesk support to quickly resolve connectivity issues for end-users.
  • Performing regular configuration backups for routers and switches to guarantee rapid disaster recovery if a piece of hardware fails.
06
Cloud & Advanced Networking
  • Creating secure, high-speed connections between on-premises infrastructure and public clouds (such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud).
  • Implementing Software-Defined Networking (SDN) to centralize and automate network management through software interfaces rather than manual hardware configuration.
  • Configuring load balancing to evenly distribute incoming network traffic across multiple servers, ensuring high availability and preventing server overloads.

Our Tool Stack

To provide robust network monitoring and security, our stack of tools can scale from a central operations hub out to multiple remote branches.

Unified Infrastructure Monitoring

Central operations facility to track uptime, bandwidth utilization, and traffic volumes across distributed networks and branch locations.

  • PRTG Network Monitor: Highly visual with a sensor-based licensing model. Creates real-time dashboards that display the health of network hardware, bandwidth usage, and environmental metrics across various sites.
  • SolarWinds Network Performance Monitor (NPM): An enterprise-grade standard that offers deep, granular visibility into network performance, automated mapping, and advanced alerting for large-scale, multi-site environments.
  • LogicMonitor: A SaaS-based, automated platform that excels at auto-discovering devices and scaling quickly, making it ideal for tracking large volumes of infrastructure and branch deployments.
SIEM (Security Information and Event Management)

A SIEM is the analytical engine of any security command center. It aggregates logs, network traffic data, and security alerts from across the entire infrastructure to detect anomalies and correlate threat data.

  • Splunk Enterprise Security: A powerhouse for ingesting massive amounts of log data and performing complex searches and visualizations to identify security incidents.
  • Microsoft Sentinel: A cloud-native SIEM that leverages AI to analyze security signals across the network, integrating seamlessly with other enterprise and cloud environments.
  • IBM QRadar: Known for its strong out-of-the-box threat detection rules and network behavior anomaly detection capabilities.
Endpoint and Network Detection (EDR / NDR)

Securing the perimeter isn't enough; an IT firm must monitor behavior down to individual devices and internal traffic flows.

  • CrowdStrike Falcon or SentinelOne: Leading EDR platforms that use AI and machine learning to detect and automatically block advanced threats, malware, and ransomware on servers and remote endpoints.
  • Darktrace: A Network Detection and Response (NDR) tool that uses self-learning AI to understand the normal "pattern of life" for a network and instantly flags or interrupts abnormal operational volumes or suspicious lateral movement.
Automated Configuration and Patch Management

Managing device configurations and patching software across numerous endpoints and branches requires automation to prevent vulnerabilities and maintain operational consistency.

  • ManageEngine Network Configuration Manager: Automates the backup of switch and router configurations, tracks changes, and ensures all devices comply with security policies.
  • Automox or NinjaOne: Cloud-native endpoint management platforms that allow IT firms to automate patch deployment and software updates remotely, minimizing the maintenance burden.
IT Service Management (ITSM) and Incident Response

When alerts trigger, the firm needs a structured way to triage, assign, and resolve the issues to maintain service level agreements.

  • ServiceNow or Jira Service Management: Enterprise-level platforms for tracking incidents, managing maintenance schedules, and coordinating response efforts between Tier 1, 2, and 3 technicians.
  • PagerDuty: An incident response platform that integrates with monitoring tools to instantly route critical alerts to the right on-call engineers, ensuring rapid response to severe outages or security breaches.
