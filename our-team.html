Senior Freelance Network & Systems Enginee

Professional Overview

With 22 years of hands-on experience, I am a senior freelance network and systems engineer who helps businesses design, build, secure, and maintain dependable IT infrastructure. My work covers the full network lifecycle — from planning IP schemes and configuring routers, switches, and firewalls, to setting up monitoring so problems are caught before they cause downtime. Alongside networking, I administer Linux servers, work with databases, and build integrations and AI-powered automations that connect systems and remove repetitive manual work. I also automate infrastructure using Terraform and Ansible, so deployments are fast, consistent and repeatable.

Over two decades I have worked across complex, production environments and learned what keeps networks stable and secure under real-world conditions. I focus on clean, well-documented configurations, security-conscious setups, and clear communication throughout a project. Whether you need a one-time deployment, ongoing managed support, or a specific problem solved quickly, I deliver reliable results and leave you with infrastructure you can trust.

    • Network redesign, segmentation and performance optimisation

    • Firewall policy and rule management, NAT, ACLs and segmentation
    • Site-to-site and remote-access VPN setup and hardening
  • Network Monitoring (NMS) — Gain visibility and prevent downtime.
    • Deploy and configure monitoring tools, SNMP/syslog, dashboards and alerts
    • Performance baselining and SLA/health reporting
  • Linux Server Administration — Set up, secure and maintain servers.
    • Installation, configuration, user and service management, log analysis
    • Shell scripting and scheduled automation
  • Database Support — Keep data organised and safe.
    • SQL queries and reporting, backups and recovery, basic administration
  • System Integration & Automation — Make tools work together.
    • API and webhook integrations, data exchange between systems
    • Automating repetitive tasks and workflows
  • Infrastructure as Code & DevOps Automation — Provision and manage infrastructure from code.
    • Terraform for provisioning servers, networks and cloud resources
    • Ansible for configuration management and automated, repeatable deployments
    • Version-controlled infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines and rollback
  • AI / LLM Workflows — Add intelligent automation.
    • Build chatbots, assistants and agents with LangChain and LangGraph
    • Retrieval (RAG) and integrating LLMs into existing systems
  • Troubleshooting & Ongoing Support — Diagnose and fix network, server and connectivity issues; available for retainer or on-demand support.
